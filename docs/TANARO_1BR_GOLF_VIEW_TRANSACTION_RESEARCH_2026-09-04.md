## Decision View

For a real golf-course / lake-view Tanaro 1BR around 736 to 756 sqft:

- Strong buy zone: under AED 1.40M if condition, view and tenancy are acceptable.
- Fair zone: AED 1.45M to AED 1.55M, especially if vacant, upgraded, furnished or strong view.
- Expensive zone: AED 1.60M+ unless the unit is clearly premium, vacant, high floor, upgraded, or has an unusually strong view.

Based on the observed last-six-month transactions, AED 1.70M is an upper asking level, not a proven recent transaction level.
