## Positioning Update

Oasis Revenue Lab should be pitched less as a one-off revenue review and more as an always-on STR revenue manager assistant:

- daily monitoring of pricing, pace, open nights, gaps, minimum stays and booking windows;
- competitor and OTA ranking checks across Airbnb, Booking.com and relevant public market signals;
- pricing and price-map writing, including recommended day-by-day adjustments;
- PriceLabs / PMS / OTA pricing support where access and approval allow;
- listing-performance review, photo/order conversion checks and owner-ready reporting;
- human revenue judgement from Damiano on top of the agent's daily scan.

For outreach, avoid claiming unsupervised automatic pricing changes. The stronger wording is: the agent can monitor daily, write the pricing logic and price maps, recommend or prepare adjustments, and support implementation with the operator's approval/control.
