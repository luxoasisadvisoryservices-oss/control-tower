# Conviva Brand Risk Review - 2026-08-08

Purpose: decide whether to keep using `conviva.live` for the Lux Oasis Advisory guest-communication product after finding an existing California software company called Conviva.

## Executive Verdict

Do not panic-change the domain today, but do not keep building `Conviva` as an unqualified standalone brand.

Recommended operating decision:

- Keep `conviva.live` live temporarily because the website and product work are already done.
- Immediately reposition it as `Conviva by Lux Oasis Advisory` or `Lux Oasis Conviva` in visible copy, decks and outreach.
- Before serious paid marketing, investor material, broad LinkedIn posting or external operator outreach, run a proper trademark/legal clearance check.
- Prepare a fallback rename now so a future change is controlled, not rushed.

This is not legal advice. It is a commercial risk assessment based on public brand overlap.

## What Was Checked

Our site:

- `https://conviva.live` resolves to `https://www.conviva.live/`.
- Current title: `Conviva - AI-Powered Guest Communication`.
- Positioning: AI-powered guest communication for holiday-home operators.
- Product story: WhatsApp-native guest replies, Chatwoot monitoring, Hostaway/PMS flow, property-specific knowledge, human handover, dashboard and Lux Oasis Advisory ownership.

Existing external company:

- `conviva.com` redirects to `conviva.ai`.
- Public positioning: real-time experience intelligence for AI agents, apps and websites.
- About-page positioning: outcome-first analytics platform for the agentic era; monitors AI agent sessions; digital product insights; video streaming insights.
- Public company footprint: Foster City / Silicon Valley software company, enterprise scale, docs, careers, newsroom and long operating history.

## Risk Assessment

Risk level: medium now, high if we scale externally under the standalone `Conviva` name.

Why:

- Same exact word mark: `Conviva`.
- Same broad software / AI space.
- Their current messaging now includes AI agents, apps, websites and travel/hospitality references in public snippets.
- Our site title says AI-powered guest communication, which is close enough to create confusion with an AI/customer-experience software company.
- Their footprint is much larger than ours, so even if our product is niche, we would be the weaker party in a brand dispute.

What helps us:

- Our product is narrow: short-term rental / holiday-home guest operations.
- Our domain is `.live`, not `.com` or `.ai`.
- We can clearly anchor it under Lux Oasis Advisory & Services LLC.
- Current market exposure appears early, so changing or qualifying the brand now is cheaper than later.

## Recommendation

Best path:

1. Keep `conviva.live` as a temporary product/demo URL.
2. Change public wording from `Conviva` alone to `Conviva by Lux Oasis Advisory` immediately.
3. Add footer/context copy: `Conviva is a Lux Oasis Advisory & Services LLC product for short-term-rental guest operations.`
4. Avoid public copy that sounds like broad AI analytics, digital experience intelligence, app analytics or agent-monitoring.
5. Prepare 3-5 replacement names before the commercial proof pack goes public.
6. Ask a UAE/IP lawyer or trademark search provider to confirm whether `Conviva` can be safely used in UAE/hospitality SaaS before committing to paid marketing or a product launch.

My practical view: if this is only a private demo, keep it. If Damiano wants to sell this as a real SaaS/advisory product, we should strongly consider renaming before the first serious operator campaign.

Avoid:

## Rename Criteria

A better name should:

- be available as a domain;
- sound hospitality/guest-ops specific;
- avoid direct collision with large software companies;
- be easy to say in Dubai operator meetings;
- work under Lux Oasis Advisory;
- leave room for supervised AI, WhatsApp, Chatwoot and PMS workflows.

Final naming should be checked for domain and trademark conflicts before use.
