## Decision

Emaar Beachfront can make sense only as a selective holiday-home management lead where all three are true:

1. Building rules and holiday-home permissions are clear.
2. The unit is furnished or easy to launch without heavy capex.
3. Rent is low enough to leave operator margin after owner/rent, furnishing, utilities, platform fees, cleaning, pricing software and staff/ops overhead.

Do not push weak Emaar Beachfront units just because the area is premium. Older workspace guardrails still apply: Emaar buildings were previously de-prioritised because rule changes may restrict holiday-home operations. Quality, presentation and rule clarity matter more than raw unit count.
