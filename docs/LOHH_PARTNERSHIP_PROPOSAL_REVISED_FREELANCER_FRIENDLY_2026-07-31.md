# Property Growth Collaboration

## Acquisition Partner Or Simple Property Finder

Prepared for: [Candidate Name]

This is a commercial discussion document for people who can help Lux Oasis Holiday Homes grow its Dubai managed-property portfolio.

Lux Oasis Holiday Homes manages short-term rental apartments in Dubai and is looking for more strong owner and property relationships, especially in JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai and selected Dubai Marina opportunities.

There are two possible ways to work together:

- **Option A: Acquisition & Operations Partner** - for someone who wants to source properties and stay involved in the owner relationship, onboarding and daily operation.
- **Option B: Simple Property Finder** - for someone who only wants to find suitable property opportunities, introduce Lux Oasis, help bring the owner to signature, and then earn a commission once the unit is signed, launched and generating revenue.

The second route is intentionally simple. A property finder is not expected to manage landlords, guests, operations, cleaning, maintenance, pricing or reporting after the property is handed to Lux Oasis.

## 1. The Opportunity

Lux Oasis Holiday Homes is growing through revenue-share and selected hybrid structures with property owners.

We are interested in:

- furnished or furnishable 1-2 bedroom apartments;
- strong short-term rental locations;
- owners open to revenue-share or approved hybrid structures;
- realistic launch timing;
- units where the numbers can work for the owner, Lux Oasis and the person who introduced the opportunity.

Priority areas:

- JBR;
- Palm Jumeirah;
- Downtown Dubai;
- selected Dubai Marina opportunities where the property and owner route are strong.

## 2. Option A - Acquisition & Operations Partner

This route is for someone who wants a bigger role.

It can include:

- finding owners, landlords, agents or investors;
- building the relationship;
- helping negotiate and move the owner to signature;
- supporting onboarding;
- helping with owner relationship and unit performance;
- staying involved in day-to-day operation if agreed.

This is not the simple freelancer route. Higher upside is possible only because the person is taking more responsibility.

Indicative structure:

| Role | What it means | Indicative upside |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition support | You source the owner/property and help bring it to signature. | 18% of Lux Oasis retained net profit for 24 months from launch |
| Acquisition + active operations | You source the unit and actively support owner relationship, onboarding, coordination and performance. | 25% while actively operating |
| Proven A-to-Z management | You prove you can manage multiple units end to end with minimal involvement from Lux Oasis. | Up to 30% after proof |

The exact structure depends on the property, role, responsibility and final agreement.

## 3. Option B - Simple Property Finder

This route is for freelancers, agents, introducers or commercially connected people who can bring suitable property opportunities but do not want an operating role.

The role is simple:

1. You find a suitable property or owner route.
2. You introduce Lux Oasis to the owner, landlord, agent or decision maker.
3. You help move the conversation to a serious meeting or signing stage.
4. Lux Oasis takes over the commercial discussion, onboarding and operations.
5. If the property signs, launches and generates collected revenue, you earn a commission from Lux Oasis retained net profit from that unit.

You do not need to:

- manage the landlord after signature;
- handle guest communication;
- manage cleaning or maintenance;
- manage pricing;
- prepare owner reports;
- operate the unit day to day;
- work under a salary or visa structure.

This route is designed for people who can open real doors. If you bring a real property that signs and launches, you share in the upside without becoming the operator.

## 4. Simple Finder Commission

For the active LinkedIn freelancer sourcing route, the standard structure is:

**20% of Lux Oasis retained net profit for 12 months from the unit launch date.**

This applies only to the specific property you personally introduce and that Lux Oasis accepts, signs and launches.

For a very strong direct-owner route or exceptional property, Lux Oasis may discuss:

- a longer commission period;
- a stronger finder commission;
- or a move into the Acquisition & Operations Partner route if you want to stay involved.

The finder route remains simple: bring the opportunity, help us reach signature, then Lux Oasis operates.

## 5. What Lux Oasis Retained Net Profit Means

Commission is calculated on Lux Oasis retained net profit from the specific unit.

Lux Oasis retained net profit means the Lux Oasis share after:

- owner payout;
- direct unit costs;
- platform / OTA adjustments;
- refunds, chargebacks and revenue adjustments;
- agreed unit-specific operating costs;
- collected revenue only.

Commission is not calculated on:

- gross booking revenue;
- owner profit;
- full property profit before owner split;
- unrelated Lux Oasis units;
- company-level profit;
- deposits, reimbursements, pass-through charges or uncollected revenue.

## 6. When Finder Commission Starts

Finder commission starts only when all of the following happen:

- you introduce a new eligible property or owner route;
- Lux Oasis accepts the introduction in writing;
- the property was not already active in the Lux Oasis pipeline;
- Lux Oasis signs the property under an approved structure;
- the unit launches; and
- the unit starts generating collected revenue.

There is no commission only for sending a public link, screenshot, portal listing or random contact unless that route leads to a signed and live Lux Oasis property.

## 7. What Lux Oasis Handles

Lux Oasis handles:

- owner-facing proposal;
- commercial discussion;
- short-term rental setup;
- listing and channel setup;
- pricing and revenue-management approach;
- guest communication standards;
- cleaning and operations coordination;
- owner reporting;
- payout process;
- final decision on whether the property is accepted.

This is why the Simple Property Finder route does not require the freelancer to manage the owner or property after signature.

## 8. Boundaries

To protect everyone, a finder cannot:

- promise income, occupancy, nightly rates or returns;
- agree owner payouts or contract terms;
- sign anything on behalf of Lux Oasis;
- collect money;
- represent themselves as a Lux Oasis employee;
- publish or market a unit under Lux Oasis without written approval;
- give legal, tax, brokerage or investment advice on behalf of Lux Oasis.

These boundaries are not meant to make the role heavy. They are there so the finder can focus on introductions while Lux Oasis handles the formal business and operating side.

## 9. Lead Protection

If Lux Oasis accepts an introduction in writing, the finder route is protected for that opportunity.

Recommended protection:

- 6 months from accepted introduction to signed agreement.

If the owner/property does not sign within that period, the introduction expires unless Lux Oasis agrees in writing to extend it.

If two people introduce the same owner or property, Lux Oasis will recognise the person who created the first useful, documented and accepted route.
