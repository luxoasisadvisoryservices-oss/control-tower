## 1. Balimmo

To: hello@balimmo-management.com  
Subject: Hybrid AI revenue-manager review for one villa

Hi Balimmo team,

I am Damiano Pesciaioli, CRME, founder of Oasis Revenue Lab. My background is in hospitality revenue management, and I now work with short-term-rental and villa operators on pricing, PriceLabs strategy, listing performance and owner-ready revenue reporting.

I am reaching out because we are building a hybrid revenue manager model for operators like you: a 24/7 AI revenue manager assistant plus my human revenue-management review.

The assistant can monitor pricing, OTA ranking position, competitor movement, calendar gaps, open nights, booking pace, minimum-stay rules and demand signals every day. It can also prepare price maps and pricing-adjustment recommendations, so your team has clear actions to review instead of only a static dashboard or monthly report.

The human part is important. This is not automatic pricing left alone. I review the strategy, pressure-test the recommendations, adjust the revenue logic and help decide when to protect ADR, when to chase occupancy, when to fill gap nights and when the issue is not price but listing conversion or OTA positioning.

For one public villa/listing, I can prepare a short free review showing 2-3 practical revenue opportunities or confirming where the setup is already strong. The lens would be pricing structure, gap-night logic, minimum-stay setup, competitor position, Airbnb/OTA ranking and listing conversion.

Nothing would be changed automatically, and there is no guaranteed uplift or pressure. The idea is simply to show how a hybrid AI + revenue-manager assistant can support daily revenue work while your team stays in control.

Would you be open to sending one public villa/listing for review?

Best,
