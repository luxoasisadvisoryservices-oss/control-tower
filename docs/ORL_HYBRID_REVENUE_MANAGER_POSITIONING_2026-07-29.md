# ORL Hybrid Revenue Manager Positioning - 2026-07-29

## Core Thesis

PriceLabs AI will make basic pricing Q&A and dashboard navigation easier for everyone.

Damiano should not position himself as "the person who clicks PriceLabs better". That layer will be automated.

The defensible position is:

> Damiano is the revenue operator who sets the commercial goal, checks the data truth, judges the trade-offs, explains the decision to owners, tracks outcomes, and uses AI/PriceLabs/PMS data as a power layer.

Oasis Revenue Lab should be the AI operating desk behind that role.

## What Damiano Should Focus On Personally

### 1. Goal Setting

Most operators do not have a clean revenue goal per listing.

Damiano should become strong at extracting:

- owner target;
- minimum acceptable owner payout;
- revenue target;
- occupancy/ADR preference;
- cash-flow pressure;
- risk appetite;
- booking-window strategy;
- what counts as success for that owner/listing.

This maps directly to PriceLabs' new goals feature, but ORL should make it operational.

### 2. Judgement

AI can suggest. Damiano must decide.

Core judgement areas:

- when to protect ADR vs chase occupancy;
- when to accept a lower rate to avoid a gap;
- when high occupancy is bad because rate is too cheap;
- when no-bookings is not a price problem;
- when owner expectations are unrealistic;
- when a market signal is weak, stale or not comparable;
- when a PriceLabs recommendation conflicts with PMS truth.

### 3. Data Truth

Revenue management fails when the numbers are wrong.

Damiano should own:

- PriceLabs vs Hostaway/PMS reconciliation;
- booked vs blocked vs available nights;
- realised revenue vs forecast;
- owner payout vs gross revenue;
- channel/source performance;
- cost floor and profitability;
- stale/missing/conflicting data flags.

### 4. Recommendation Accountability

Most tools recommend, then forget.

Damiano/ORL should track:

- recommendation made;
- reason/evidence;
- action taken or rejected;
- expected impact;
- actual outcome;
- lesson for next time.

This is the layer that makes the service defensible.

### 5. Owner Communication

Owners do not buy "AI pricing".

They buy:

- confidence;
- clear explanation;
- fewer surprises;
- better owner reports;
- proof that someone is watching the asset.

Damiano should use AI to prepare owner-ready explanations, then apply final judgement before sending.

## What ORL Should Do Behind Damiano

### Daily

- Pull PriceLabs/PMS state.
- Identify listings off pace against goal.
- Detect no-booking / low-pace / over-discount / gap-night issues.
- Draft the daily command card.
- Create approval cards for price/min-stay/promo/action changes.

### Weekly

- Review goal progress by listing.
- Review day-of-week performance.
- Review booking source/channel mix.
- Check listing/ranking/visibility problems.
- Update the recommendation ledger.

### Monthly

- Produce owner-ready report.
- Explain target vs actual.
- Show actions taken and outcomes.
- Recommend next month strategy.

### On Demand

- Answer "why is this not booking?"
- Build scenario plans.
- Compare options.
- Draft owner explanations.
- Draft price-action approvals.

## Product/Service Position

Best commercial structure:

1. Revenue Leak Audit
   - One listing.
   - Find gap to goal, pricing/visibility/listing issues and next actions.

2. Revenue Manager Plus
   - Damiano-supervised done-for-you revenue management.
   - AI monitors and drafts; Damiano approves and explains.

3. AI Revenue Desk
   - Later software layer.
   - Daily command cards, Q&A, reports, recommendation ledger and approval-gated actions for operators.

## What Not To Focus On

- Trying to beat PriceLabs at raw pricing data.
- Building a generic PriceLabs chatbot.
- Building a big dashboard before the daily decision loop works.
- Manually checking every page without converting it into a decision framework.
- Selling "AI" as the main value instead of revenue judgement and outcomes.

## Defensible Human Role

The irreplaceable part is not clicking tools.

It is:

- understanding the owner's goal;
- knowing what the data can and cannot prove;
- making a commercial judgement;
- communicating it clearly;
- taking accountability for the result;
- improving the model with each outcome.

ORL should amplify this role, not replace it.

## Next ORL Build Priority

Build the "Goal-to-Action Loop":

1. Capture goal per listing.
2. Pull PriceLabs/PMS state.
3. Calculate pace and gap.
4. Diagnose reason.
5. Recommend action.
6. Ask for approval where needed.
7. Track outcome.
8. Produce owner explanation.
