# Syed 50-60 Property Model - 2026-07-29

## Context

Damiano asked for a rough calculation for the Syed property-acquisition model if Lux Oasis Holiday Homes reaches 50-60 properties.

Saved commercial guardrails:

- Standard Syed / BD commercial range was 15-25% depending on role and property economics.
- Current Syed exception: 35% of Lux Oasis retained net profit for acquisition-only.
- Current Syed exception: up to 50% only while Syed genuinely manages the unit A-to-Z.
- 70/30 applies only to separately charged and collected brokerage/agency commission personally sourced/closed by Syed, not rental revenue or recurring Lux Oasis retained profit.
- Syed earns only on units or brokerage deals he personally sources/closes and, where applicable, actively manages.

## Assumptions

- Lux Oasis retained net profit before Syed, based on saved model:
  - Average current-style unit: AED 2,525/month.
  - Good unit: AED 3,500/month.
  - Strong unit: AED 5,000/month.
- Incremental scaling software/ops allowance: AED 150/unit/month.
- Current fixed overhead baseline: AED 6,000/month.
- Operating profit means after Syed share, incremental unit allowance and fixed overhead.
- Founder/business split: 50% draw to Damiano, 50% retained/reinvested in Lux Oasis.

## Readout

With 50-60 average current-style units:

- At 35% acquisition-only, the business can likely produce about AED 69k-83k/month operating profit, giving Damiano and retained business cash about AED 34k-42k/month each.
- At 50% A-to-Z, the business can likely produce about AED 50k-61k/month operating profit, giving Damiano and retained business cash about AED 25k-30k/month each.

Good units change the picture materially: 50-60 good units can produce about AED 74k-122k/month operating profit depending on Syed's role/share.

Conclusion: 50-60 properties can be very meaningful, but only if quality stays controlled and Syed's 50% tier is tied strictly to real A-to-Z operating responsibility. If Lux Oasis later adds a junior ops manager or takes operations back, Syed should step down to acquisition-only economics from that handover date.
