## Prep Adjustment

For final CRME practice, use this format:

- 50 questions;
- 60 minutes;
- 5 answer options, A to E;
- mostly conceptual and scenario questions;
- answer key after completion;
- explanations only in review mode, not on the timed version.

The key exam habit is to choose the most strategic answer: broad business alignment, profitability, market context, collaboration, sustainability, technology as support, and long-term value over narrow tactical answers.
