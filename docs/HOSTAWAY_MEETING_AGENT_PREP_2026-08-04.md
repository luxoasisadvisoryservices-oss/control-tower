# Hostaway Meeting Agent Prep - 2026-08-04

## Purpose

Prepare the Lux Oasis side for a likely Hostaway meeting next week. This is not only Lin's call. Lin brings daily-use operator feedback; the COO agent prepares workflow, automation and reporting questions.

## Meeting Goal

Find out whether Hostaway can reduce manual work and improve direct-booking operations for Lux Oasis Holiday Homes, especially around booking creation, payment/status tracking, guest details, agreements, calendar/access sync and useful reporting.

## Lin Feedback Needed

- What is not straightforward in daily Hostaway use.
- Where adding a direct booking is slow or confusing.
- Payment/status fields that are hard to track.
- Guest-detail and rental-agreement pain points.
- Calendar, Keyless and access-management issues.
- Manual work she repeats that Hostaway should simplify.

## Operational Pain Points

- Forwarded Hostaway review notifications can be missed if the check only looks for direct Hostaway sender matches.
- Bed Setup and same-day readiness are not just about beds: they can also depend on rental agreement, guest guide, passports/DTCM, keyless access and final turnover timing.
- Final readiness can still have an open Lin `done/blocker` item even after one sub-step is marked complete.
- Direct booking handling still needs a clean path from enquiry to confirmed stay, especially when dates, access and payment status change.
- Reporting needs to be usable by operators and owners, not just visible inside Hostaway.

## Agent-Side Questions

1. Direct booking creation: what is the cleanest workflow for adding a direct booking from first enquiry to confirmed stay?
2. Payment tracking: how should Lux Oasis track paid, unpaid, partial, charge failed, deposit and balance states?
3. Rental agreement: can Hostaway support a structured agreement flow, signature status and document storage per reservation?
4. Guest details: can required guest data be captured in a way that is exportable and visible before arrival?
5. Calendar and access sync: what is the best way to connect Hostaway date changes to Keyless/access validity?
6. Automation/API: what API, webhook or Zapier events are available for new bookings, modified dates, payment changes and guest messages?
7. Reporting: can Hostaway provide owner-ready reporting by property, booking source, revenue, fees and payouts?
8. Alerts: can urgent items be pushed reliably to Slack/email so Lin does not miss guest or booking events?
9. Review monitoring: can Hostaway surface review notifications and forwarded guest messages in a way that does not depend on direct sender matching?
