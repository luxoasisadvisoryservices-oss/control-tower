# Nest Match Founder-Led LinkedIn GTM

Created: 2026-08-22 21:58 Dubai

## Decision

Nest Match should be promoted through a founder-led community build, not mainly through the Lux Oasis Advisory company page.

The Lux Oasis Advisory & Services LinkedIn page is the parent credibility hub for ORL, Oasis Guest Lab, Nest Match and Renovation. It should publish proof and ecosystem updates. It should not be the main cold-outreach identity for Nest Match at seed stage.

Damiano's personal founder profile should be the relationship engine.

## Why

Kunjey's founder-led approach is strong because early B2B networks sell through trust, not polished corporate branding. Holiday-home companies are more likely to respond to another operator/founder asking for founding feedback than to a new company page asking them to sign up.

Nest Match is a marketplace/network. At seed stage, it needs real people, not generic followers.

## Positioning

Use:

> I am building Nest Match as a private Dubai STR operator network: inventory, trusted intros, service partners and operator-safe contract checklists in one place.

Avoid:

- "We are launching a marketplace."
- "Join our platform."
- "We are like Kunjey."
- "We already have live inventory."
- "We are better than Hostaway/Guesty/Kunjey."

## Channel Roles

### Damiano Personal LinkedIn

Primary for:

- founder-to-founder introductions;
- operator feedback;
- warm comments;
- direct relationship building;
- beta invitations;
- thought-leadership posts from the operator perspective.

### Lux Oasis Advisory & Services LinkedIn

Primary for:

- umbrella credibility;
- weekly ecosystem updates;
- ORL / Oasis Guest Lab / Nest Match proof posts;
- founder story reposts;
- safe product education.

### Nest Match Website / Page

Primary for:

- early-access capture;
- private-beta explanation;
- user role clarity;
- credibility after someone clicks.

## Target Segments

1. Holiday-home operators
   - ask for founding feedback;
   - focus on operators who understand gap nights, owner leads, agents and service-provider problems.

2. Service providers
   - cleaning, maintenance, furnishing, photography, laundry, linens, access, compliance;
   - pitch as visibility to verified holiday-home operators, not as SaaS.

3. Real estate agents / brokers
   - pitch as a way to route suitable units or accommodation requirements to verified operators.

4. Owners / landlords
   - later, after operator and service-provider sides have proof.

The idea is simple: verified operators, agents and service providers should have one cleaner place to find inventory, trusted intros, service partners and operator-safe checklists instead of relying only on scattered WhatsApp groups.

I am not doing a broad launch yet. I am speaking with a small group of Dubai operators first to understand what would actually be useful. Would you be open to giving me 10 minutes of honest feedback as a founding operator?

## Operating Rule

Use the founder-led conversation to collect the first 20 verified operators/service providers. Use the company page to make the project look credible after they check Damiano's profile. Do not rely on the company page to create the first liquidity.
