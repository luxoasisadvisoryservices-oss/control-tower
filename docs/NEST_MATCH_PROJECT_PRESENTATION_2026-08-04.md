# Nest Match Project Presentation - 2026-08-04

Audience: banker, funding partner, advisor, beta user, operator, service provider, or early strategic partner.

## Slide 1 - Nest Match

The private operator network for short-term rentals.

Dubai first. App-ready. Controlled private beta.

## Slide 2 - The Problem

Short-term-rental execution runs through scattered chats.

- Operators look for owners, agents, cleaners, maintenance teams and suppliers through WhatsApp, Telegram, referrals and old contact lists.
- Owners and agents struggle to find serious operators and execution partners.
- Service providers have availability, but no clean route to trusted operators.
- Opportunities move quickly, but trust, verification and follow-up stay manual.

## Slide 3 - The Product

Nest Match is a structured network for the short-term-rental ecosystem.

- Inventory Board for units, empty nights, operator requests and service needs.
- Role-based profiles for operators, owners, agents and service providers.
- Direct messaging and introductions.
- Service-provider directory.
- Contract checklist and template hub, kept informational until legal review.
- Manual beta approval before wider access.

## Slide 4 - Why Dubai First

Dubai is the proof market.

- Large and active holiday-home market.
- Fragmented operators, landlords, agents and vendors.
- Real operational pain already visible through Lux Oasis Holiday Homes.
- Existing substitutes are fragmented chats, directories and manual referrals.
- Local trust matters, so liquidity should be built city by city.

## Slide 5 - What Makes It Different

Nest Match is not just a directory.

- It connects supply, demand, services and trust signals in one place.
- It is built around active operator workflow, not only profile listings.
- It can support owners, agents, operators and vendors in the same local network.
- It is designed to become mobile-first because the market already works from phones.
- Dubai is first, but the architecture should support other STR cities later.

## Slide 6 - Business Model

Start with controlled access and useful liquidity before pushing monetisation.

- Free tier for first conversations and network participation.
- Pro plan for more contact credits, visibility, saved alerts and attachments.
- Premium plan for higher visibility, larger contact limits, team/company profile and advanced filters.
- Future revenue can include sponsored visibility, verified provider upgrades, business dashboards and contract/checklist tools.

## Slide 7 - Current Status

Good enough for controlled beta signal. Not ready for broad paid launch.

Working or positive:
- Homepage positioning is focused on private beta and Dubai first.
- Email confirmation, basic role dashboards, two-sided text messaging and basic Inventory Board posting have previously passed QA.
- Legal footer says the platform is a marketplace only, not a broker, property manager or legal advisor.

Still blocked:
- Direct payment-success pages must be session-verified.
- Browse route has a live database/Supabase error.
- Some logged-in routes still need fix or hiding.
- Full landlord, operator, service-provider and admin role QA still needs a fresh pass.

## Slide 8 - Launch Plan

Launch controlled, not broad.

1. Fix P0 safety blockers.
2. Keep public site as early-access/private beta signal.
3. Invite 20-30 curated Dubai STR users.
4. Seed operators, service providers and owner/agent-side contacts manually.
5. Measure repeat use: posts, replies, saved contacts, intros and service requests.
6. Widen only after the core loop works.

## Slide 9 - Funding Use

Funding should support proof, not vanity launch.

Acceptable use:
- Product safety fixes and QA.
- Controlled beta operations.
- Verified Supabase/database, auth, storage and monitoring when real traffic justifies it.
- Targeted founder-led beta acquisition, not paid traffic.

Not acceptable:
- Broad marketing before safety blockers close.
- Borrowing for cosmetic polish.
- Heavy personal guarantees or repayment pressure that weakens Lux Oasis cash buffers.

## Slide 10 - The Ask

The current ask is not "fund a finished marketplace".

It is:

- support a controlled Dubai beta;
- validate the core operator network loop;
- keep legal, payment and data-risk surfaces gated;
- expand city by city only after real marketplace activity exists.

One-line answer:

Nest Match is building the coordination layer missing from short-term rentals: inventory, trusted intros, service providers and operator-safe checklists in one private network.
