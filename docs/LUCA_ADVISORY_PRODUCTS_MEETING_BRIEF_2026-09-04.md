Meeting date: Friday, 4 September 2026

## Executive Direction

The meeting centred on three Lux Oasis Advisory product lanes that could eventually form one connected STR operator ecosystem:

- Oasis Guest Lab: guest operations AI for short-term rentals.
- Revenue Assist: AI-assisted revenue-management service and product layer.
- Nest Match: STR marketplace connecting landlords, agents, operators and service providers.

The commercial direction is to avoid presenting unfinished products as fully launched marketplaces. Each product should be moved through proof, controlled beta, and only then broader public positioning.

## Product Roles

### Oasis Guest Lab

Oasis Guest Lab should be positioned as an AI concierge and operations assistant for short-term-rental teams, supporting VAs rather than replacing them.

Core scope:

- Guest messaging.
- Maintenance follow-ups.
- Check-in and check-out coordination.
- Cleaning coordination.
- Editable property policies.
- Editable AI tone.
- WhatsApp, Airbnb, Booking.com and PMS integration.
- Launch video and product demo as near-term sales assets.

### Revenue Assist

Revenue Assist should be developed as a supervised AI revenue-management layer rather than only a PriceLabs wrapper.

Core scope:

- Telegram interface.
- Dashboards.
- Daily reports.
- Market research and competitor checks.
- PriceLabs and other pricing-software integrations.
- Credit/subscription pricing options.
- API and token cost control.
- Stress testing for concurrent users and properties.
- Product stability for resale or white-label partnerships.

### Nest Match

Nest Match should become a Dubai-first STR ecosystem marketplace.

Target participants:

- Landlords.
- Real-estate agents.
- Operators.
- Service providers.

Core principles:

- Reviews and reputation system matter.
- Anti-spam matching should require landlord approval.
- Landlord access should be free.
- Providers and agents can have free and paid tiers.
- Dubai first, international later.
- Keep both paths open: lifestyle business or future acquisition/exit.

## Nest Match Launch Sequence

Do not launch Nest Match broadly into Airbnb/operator groups while it is still an empty shell.

Recommended sequence:

1. Seed supply first with real service-provider and operator profiles.
2. Start with existing contacts and trusted providers.
3. Add agents after there is credible provider/operator supply.
4. Approach landlords later, once the marketplace looks useful.
5. Use scraping to create draft provider profiles only where legally and reputationally acceptable.
6. Contact providers with ready-made claimable pages.
7. Use LinkedIn carefully, but do not rely on it as the only channel.
8. Launch into WhatsApp operator groups only after enough visible listings and credible profiles exist.

Current tactical rule: in operator groups, frame demand testing softly as a vetted provider list or private STR support network, not as a fully launched marketplace.
