## Market Logic

NYC and Singapore are useful, but neither should be treated like normal unrestricted Airbnb host outreach.

- NYC: focus on legal short-term, furnished, corporate-housing, serviced-apartment and professional STR operators. Avoid implying support for non-compliant private short stays.
- Singapore: focus on serviced apartments, aparthotels, flexible living, corporate housing and regional Asia villa/property managers. Private residential Airbnb-style short stays are regulation-sensitive.
- Bali remains useful because villa operators are easier to match to the free one-listing revenue review.
- Rome/Milan remains useful only where the operator is small enough to need a second revenue-management brain and not already a polished professional RM shop.

## Offer Angle

Use the same core ORL offer across markets:

- Hybrid AI + human revenue-manager support.
- Daily checks on pricing, gaps, booking pace, OTA visibility, ranking where available, minimum stays, competitor movement and listing conversion.
- One free public listing/unit review as the door opener.
- No automatic pricing changes.
- No guaranteed uplift.
- Operator stays in control.

For NYC and Singapore, add compliance-safe wording:

> I am looking only at legal serviced/furnished/short-stay inventory and public listing performance. I am not suggesting any change to your operating model or local compliance setup.
