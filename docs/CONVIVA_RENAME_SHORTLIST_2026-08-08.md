## Why This Direction

`Oasis Guest Lab` is now the selected working direction because it fits the Lux Oasis Advisory family beside `Oasis Revenue Lab`.

Why it works:

- matches the `Oasis Revenue Lab` family;
- sounds AI/product-native with `.ai`;
- makes Conviva feel like part of a product ecosystem, not a random separate brand;
- fits a supervised guest-communication product better than a hard SaaS name;
- avoids the California Conviva conflict;
- can still be explained clearly as guest communication and guest operations.

Main weakness:

- `Lab` is less instantly descriptive than `Desk`, so the byline and tagline must do the practical work.

Best public line:

> Oasis Guest Lab by Lux Oasis Advisory
>
> Supervised guest communication for short-term-rental operators.

`Oasis GuestOps` remains the strongest operator-language backup:

- says what the product does: guest operations;
- fits the Lux Oasis / Oasis Revenue Lab naming family;
- avoids sounding like a broad Silicon Valley AI analytics platform;
- is easier to explain to short-term-rental operators;
- supports the current supervised-mode product truth: WhatsApp replies, Chatwoot monitoring, Hostaway/PMS context, unit knowledge and human handover.

## Recommended Shortlist

### 1. Oasis Guest Lab

Best if the goal is to create a clean Lux Oasis Advisory product family with Oasis Revenue Lab.

Use if we want the brand architecture to feel like:

- Oasis Revenue Lab = revenue/pricing intelligence.
- Oasis Guest Lab = guest communication/operations intelligence.

Website title:

> Oasis Guest Lab - Supervised Guest Communication for Short-Term Rentals

One-line pitch:

> Oasis Guest Lab helps operators reply faster to routine guest messages using unit-specific knowledge, with human handover when the answer is sensitive.

### 2. Oasis GuestOps

Best if the goal is maximum clarity for operators.

Use if we want a product name that is clear, brand-family aligned and commercially simple.

Website title:

> Oasis GuestOps - Supervised Guest Communication for Short-Term Rentals

One-line pitch:

> Oasis GuestOps replies to routine guest messages from unit-specific knowledge and escalates sensitive cases to your team.

### 3. Lux GuestOps

Good if we want stronger Lux Oasis ownership from the first word.

Pros:

- clearly belongs to Lux Oasis;
- short;
- less likely to be confused with a generic SaaS brand.

Cons:

- `Lux` is broad and already used by many unrelated brands;
- slightly less natural than `Oasis GuestOps`.

### 4. Oasis HostOps

Good but more internal/operator-facing than guest-facing.

Pros:

- strong operations feel;
- fits STR operators.

Cons:

- sounds broader than guest communication;
- could later be confused with owner acquisition, housekeeping or maintenance workflows.

### 5. Oasis Reply

Simple and understandable.

Pros:

- clear connection to messaging;
- easier for operators to understand quickly.

Cons:

- narrower than the actual product, which includes dashboard, escalation and unit knowledge;
- sounds more like a feature than a platform.

### 6. GuestBridge by Lux Oasis Advisory

Potentially good if we want a more human/service feel.

Pros:

- implies AI + human handover;
- good for supervised mode.

Cons:

- less tied to the Oasis brand;
- `GuestBridge` may be harder to own strongly.
