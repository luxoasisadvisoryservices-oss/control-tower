Video transcript review, not a live account audit. No pricing, subscription, website integration or outreach has been activated. Roadmap announcements require current availability checks.

Priority: reconcile the final prices guests pay; evaluate an owner revenue-estimator landing page; strengthen ORL reporting and safety controls.

1. Pricing and end-price audit

Trace PriceLabs → PMS → channel or direct website → guest checkout → expected net proceeds.

  • Check base, minimum and maximum prices; seasonality, demand, last-minute, far-out and occupancy adjustments.
  • Validate overrides: currency versus percentage, sign, dates, listing scope and expiry. Check minimum stays, gap nights and sync freshness.
  • Reconcile channel markups, length-of-stay discounts, promotions, cleaning/extra-guest fees, taxes and mandatory charges. Separate guest total, payout and net contribution without double-counting deductions.
  • Test short, weekly and monthly stays; weekday/weekend; near/far dates; low/peak/event periods; different guest counts. Treat unavailable combinations as bookability findings.
  • Acceptance: every price difference explained, no unintended discount stacking, no breach of the approved economic floor, and credible maximum prices.

2. Website Revenue Estimator

The video describes a Revenue Estimator (RE) widget, not a RERA tool. Proposed placement: an owner-acquisition page, separate from guest checkout.

  • Choose the exact domain and whether the page serves property-management or revenue-management enquiries.
  • Confirm vendor activation/domain whitelisting and obtain the supported embed. Verify Dubai address matching, currency, estimate assumptions and mobile behaviour.
  • Verify lead-capture availability and destination, consent/privacy, access controls, retention, spam protection and failure handling.
  • Test a submission through to the intended private lead system before launch. Do not automatically contact leads.
  • Show estimated revenue as an estimate, not guaranteed earnings or owner take-home.

Keep three integrations separate: owner earnings estimates; guest booking rates; website search-demand signals. An estimator embed does not connect booking prices.
