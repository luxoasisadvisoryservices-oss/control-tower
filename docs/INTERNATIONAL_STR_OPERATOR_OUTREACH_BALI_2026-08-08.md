## Target Filter

Prioritise:

- Bali villa or STR operators with roughly 5-30 villas/listings;
- Canggu, Seminyak, Uluwatu, Ubud, Pererenan and Bukit Peninsula;
- WhatsApp-heavy guest operations;
- visible OTA / direct-booking activity;
- operators with good product but possible weak pricing discipline, guest-message scaling pain, reporting gaps, or inconsistent listing conversion.

Avoid first:

- huge polished operators with mature internal systems;
- ultra-luxury estate brands;
- generic real-estate sales brokers with no STR operations signal;
- low-proof social-only pages where contact and operator status are unclear.
