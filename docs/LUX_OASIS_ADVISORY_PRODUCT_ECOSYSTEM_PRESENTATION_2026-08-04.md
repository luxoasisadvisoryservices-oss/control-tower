# Lux Oasis Advisory Product Ecosystem Presentation - 2026-08-04

Audience: banker, funding partner, advisor, operator, beta user, commercial partner, or early client.

## Slide 1 - Lux Oasis Advisory Product Ecosystem

Practical tools, AI workflows and operating systems for short-term-rental operators.

Core products:
- Conviva
- Oasis Revenue Lab
- Nest Match

## Slide 2 - The Market Problem

Short-term-rental operators are overloaded by daily operational decisions.

- Guest communication is fast, repetitive and sensitive.
- Revenue management needs judgement, not only dashboards.
- Property opportunities, service providers and operator relationships are scattered across chats.
- Owners want clarity, proof and fewer surprises.
- Small operators need better systems without hiring a full internal tech team.

## Slide 3 - The Umbrella

Lux Oasis Advisory & Services LLC is the product and advisory company.

It is separate from Lux Oasis Holiday Homes LLC, which is the operating proof business.

Lux Oasis Advisory turns real operating pain from holiday homes into reusable services and products:
- guest operations;
- revenue management;
- marketplace coordination;
- operating dashboards and agent workflows.

## Slide 4 - Conviva

Supervised guest communication for short-term-rental operators.

Conviva helps operators manage WhatsApp-style guest operations using property-specific knowledge and escalation.

It is designed to:
- answer common guest questions from unit knowledge;
- support check-in, access, parking, amenities and maintenance questions;
- escalate risky or unclear situations;
- keep humans in control where judgement is needed;
- give operators visibility through a dashboard.

## Slide 5 - Oasis Revenue Lab

Revenue judgement, owner-ready reporting and daily decision support.

Oasis Revenue Lab is not a PriceLabs clone. PriceLabs is the pricing engine. ORL is the operating layer that turns PriceLabs, PMS truth, owner goals and Damiano's judgement into decisions.

It is designed to:
- capture revenue goals per listing;
- compare PriceLabs data with Hostaway/PMS truth;
- diagnose pace, gaps, visibility and listing-health issues;
- create daily command cards;
- track recommendations and outcomes;
- produce owner-ready reports.

## Slide 6 - Nest Match

The private operator network for short-term rentals.

Nest Match is the marketplace and coordination layer above the ecosystem.

It is designed to connect:
- operators;
- owners and landlords;
- agents;
- service providers;
- property opportunities;
- inventory and empty-night signals.

Dubai is the first proof market. The product should be global-capable, but liquidity should launch city by city.

## Slide 7 - How The Three Products Connect

Each product solves a different daily STR pain.

- Conviva: guest operations and communication.
- Oasis Revenue Lab: revenue decisions and owner reporting.
- Nest Match: market coordination and trusted network.

Together they form a practical operating ecosystem for short-term-rental businesses.

The shared advantage is that each product is built from real Lux Oasis operating pain, not from abstract software theory.

## Slide 8 - Current Status

The ecosystem is real, but each product has different readiness.

Conviva:
- Hostaway/data path is no longer the main blocker.

Oasis Revenue Lab:

Nest Match:

## Slide 9 - Commercial Path

Start service-led, then productise what repeats.

Conviva:
- controlled pilot for 1-5 units;
- supervised guest-ops support;
- per-unit monthly pricing after proof.

Oasis Revenue Lab:
- revenue leak audit;
- Revenue Manager Plus service;
- later AI Revenue Desk for operators.

Nest Match:
- controlled Dubai beta;
- free first conversations;
- Pro/Premium visibility and contact tools after liquidity exists.

## Slide 10 - Safety And Trust

The products touch reputation, money and guest experience, so trust gates matter.

Guardrails:
- no autonomous refunds, discounts or sensitive guest promises;
- no live pricing changes without approval;
- no legal-template claims without legal review;
- no fake payment success or unverified subscription status;
- no public screenshots with private guest data;
- no broad traffic before core product loops pass QA.
