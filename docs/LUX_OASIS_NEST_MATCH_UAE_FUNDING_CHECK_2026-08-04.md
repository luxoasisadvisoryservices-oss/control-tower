# Lux Oasis / Nest Match UAE Funding Check - 2026-08-04

Purpose: quick eligibility check after Damiano forwarded a Dubai startup/SME funding screenshot.

## Verdict

Treat this as a funding lead to verify, not as approved capital.

Best fit is Lux Oasis Holiday Homes as an operating SME if the company can show licence, bank account, clean statements, revenue and a sensible use of funds.

Nest Match is a weaker normal-loan candidate until beta/product proof is cleaner. It may fit startup or digitisation routes, but not broad borrowing for speculative product spend.

## Routes Checked

### Emirates Development Bank

Relevant because EDB advertises UAE startup financing and SME financing.

Observed public terms:
- startup financing up to AED 2 million, subject to financial and qualitative evaluation;
- SME finance includes long-term finance, technology adoption, digitisation finance, project finance and credit guarantee support through partner banks;
- business banking/account support is available for SMEs.

Likely fit:
- Lux Oasis Holiday Homes: possible, if revenue and statements support repayment.
- Lux Oasis Advisory / Nest Match: possible only if positioned as technology/digitisation/product development with credible plan and proof.

Risks to check before applying:
- personal guarantee;
- collateral or assignment of receivables;
- processing fees;
- early repayment penalties;
- minimum trading history and turnover;
- whether holiday-home management or property-related services are within accepted sectors.

### Dubai Founders HQ / Dubai SME / Mohammed Bin Rashid Fund

Relevant as Dubai's startup/SME ecosystem entry point.

Important limitation: The Fund is presented as supporting Emirati entrepreneurs. Third-party summaries also describe Dubai SME seed/guarantee programmes as UAE-national owned and managed. This is probably not the fastest route for Damiano unless there is already a genuine UAE national founder/shareholder structure.

### Khalifa Fund

Funding products can be attractive on paper, including interest-free loans and grace periods, but public eligibility says Emirati entrepreneurs aged 21-60 with operational Abu Dhabi / Western Region businesses.

Likely fit: not a priority for Damiano's current Dubai business structure.

### Private SME Lenders / Bank Loans

Relevant only if terms are clean and repayment pressure does not weaken cheque buffers.

Typical bank-style requirements found in UAE funding guidance:
- UAE trade licence;
- Emirates ID/residency and KYC;
- active business bank account;
- corporate tax registration;
- clean financials;
- often 6-12 months revenue minimum, with traditional banks often preferring longer history and stronger turnover.

Likely fit:
- Lux Oasis Holiday Homes: possible working-capital or growth-finance route if DSCR and cash buffer are healthy.
- Nest Match: poor fit unless there is revenue, committed users, invoices, or a specific recoverable asset spend.

## Decision Rule

Do not borrow for Nest Match broad marketing, speculative development or cosmetic launch spend.

Consider funding only for:
- a profitable Lux Oasis property acquisition/launch with repayment covered by conservative net cash flow;
- revenue-generating operating capacity where the loan payment is comfortably covered;
- specific technology/digitisation spend tied to near-term paid users or operating savings.

## Next Action

If Damiano wants to pursue it, prepare one-page funding pack:
- company licence/entity route;
- last 6-12 months bank statements and revenue summary;
- use of funds;
- conservative repayment scenario;
- worst-case cash-buffer impact;
- terms red-line checklist.

No application, document sharing, guarantee, bank change or financial commitment without explicit approval.
