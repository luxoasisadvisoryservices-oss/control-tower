# Lux Oasis BD / Property Acquisition Visa Hire Decision - 2026-08-19

## Correction

This is about a business-development / property-acquisition person for Lux Oasis Holiday Homes, not a software developer.

The previous developer-hire note from 2026-08-19 is superseded for this question.

## Short Verdict

This can be a good position, but only if it is a property-acquisition vehicle, not a normal employee cost.

Do not give visa + salary + high percentage upfront.

Best structure:

- 30-60 day proof period first.
- Candidate must bring real owner / landlord / agent routes.
- Visa only after proof.
- Percentage must be calculated only on Lux Oasis retained net profit, not gross revenue, owner profit or full property profit.

## Visa And Setup Cost

Working budget per sponsored BD / acquisition person:

| Item | Planning range |
| --- | ---: |
| Employment visa, medical, Emirates ID, admin / PRO | AED 6,000-9,000 |
| Basic health insurance | AED 1,000-3,000 |
| Buffer for typing, amendments, document issues | AED 1,000-2,000 |
| Total first-year setup budget | AED 8,000-12,000 |

This is technically affordable for one person. The danger is not the visa fee. The danger is sponsoring someone who does not produce units.

## Salary Range

Practical monthly salary ranges:

| Role level | Salary planning range |
| --- | ---: |
| Junior acquisition runner / property coordinator | AED 3,000-5,000 |
| BD / acquisition executive with agent-owner network | AED 5,000-8,000 |
| Strong BD + operations person | AED 8,000-12,000 |

If Lux Oasis gives visa and salary, the percentage must be lower.

## Percentage Structure

Use Lux Oasis retained net profit only.

Lux Oasis retained net profit means the Lux Oasis slice after owner payout, OTA/platform costs, cleaning/linen, DTCM, refunds/claims, approved unit costs, direct expenses and collected cash.

Do not calculate on:

- gross booking revenue;
- full property net profit;
- owner share;
- company-wide profit;
- revenue before costs are settled.

Recommended structures:

| Structure | Visa / salary | Acquisition-only | Source + active operations |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Freelance / no visa / no salary | No fixed cost | 20-35% of LOHH retained net profit | 35-50% while actively operating |
| Sponsored with low salary | Visa + AED 3k-5k/month | 15-25% of LOHH retained net profit | 25-35% while actively operating |
| Sponsored with normal BD salary | Visa + AED 5k-8k/month | 10-20% of LOHH retained net profit | 20-30% while actively operating |

Standard starting offer:

- 60-day trial with no visa.
- 20% of Lux Oasis retained net profit for accepted properties the person personally introduces, signs, launches and generates collected revenue.
- Pay for 12 months from launch, not forever.
- Step up only if they also handle onboarding and operations.

Visa step-up after proof:

- If sponsored and paid a salary, use 15-20% for acquisition-only.
- If sponsored and they truly manage the unit A-to-Z, use 25-30%.
- 35-40% with visa is only for a very strong person who can bring and operate several units.
- 50% is for no-salary / no-visa or exceptional A-to-Z cases, not the standard sponsored employee model.

## What They Must Prove Before Visa

Candidate must show:

1. 5-10 real owner / landlord / agent routes, not generic portal leads.
2. 1-2 units moved to serious owner interest, economics, viewing or document stage.
3. Weekly pipeline report.
4. Ability to communicate professionally with agents / owners.
5. Ability to reduce Damiano's workload, not create more chasing.

## Why The Role Can Be Good

Lux Oasis needs more units. A good BD / acquisition person can become a growth engine if they:

- open owner and agent relationships;
- find JBR / Marina / Palm / Downtown opportunities;
- move deals to signed management agreements;
- support onboarding and launch;
- help build repeatable acquisition flow.

One extra good unit can produce roughly AED 2,500-5,000/month of Lux Oasis retained profit before partner share, depending on property quality and deal structure.

At the current average model around AED 2,525/month retained profit per unit before partner share:

- 20% partner share leaves Lux Oasis about AED 2,020/month.
- 30% partner share leaves Lux Oasis about AED 1,768/month.
- A fixed salary must be justified by multiple new units, not one weak lead.

## Decision

Good position to explore: yes.

Good to sponsor immediately: no.

Best move now:

- Recruit / test one BD acquisition person.
- Offer a 60-day proof period.
- Start with 20% of Lux Oasis retained net profit for accepted signed units, paid for 12 months after launch and collected revenue.
- Keep visa as the reward after proof, not the opening gift.
- If visa + salary is required from day one, cap fixed salary low and reduce percentage.

## Red Lines

- No visa before proof unless Damiano explicitly decides this person is exceptional.
- No percentage of gross revenue.
- No percentage of owner profit or full property profit.
- No company equity.
- No passive forever commission.
- No stacking high percentage plus separate helper salary on average-margin units.
