## Targeting rules

- “Freelancer” or “individual” is a provider type, not a service category.
- Use the exact service category first, then label the provider as company or individual freelancer.
- Include only an exact LinkedIn company/person profile with public evidence matching identity, service/role and Dubai coverage.
- Use one provider per category first in each five-contact packet.
- Hold a slot open rather than padding with generic LinkedIn searches or uncertain identities.
- The two 4/5 categories are deliberate shortfalls pending another verified contact.
